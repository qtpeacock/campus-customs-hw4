# Campus Customs Storefront Design

We restyled the site like a real Yale shop: Caslon serif headlines and prices with clean Inter text and varsity-style labels, a shield "CC" logo with a header that slims and frosts as you scroll, navy-and-white varsity stripes, and a "Since the 1970s" seal. Product cards now zoom on hover, show honest stock badges like "Only 2 left in L," offer one-click Quick add by size, and swap to a real photo of the item being worn on campus where we have one (with a photo toggle on the product page), while new home page tiles and the Products menu's Type and Collection dropdowns let shoppers browse by Residential Colleges, Sports & Teams, Yale Family, Grad Schools, or Classic Yale. Together these make the store feel trustworthy and on-brand, help shoppers find their corner of Yale in a click or two, and shorten the path from "I like that" to "it's in my cart," which keeps people browsing longer and makes them more likely to buy.
